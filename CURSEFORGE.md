# Mythic+ Timer and Tools

A Mythic+ timer with handy extra tools. It shows your key timer and helper tools while you run a dungeon. Everything can be turned on or off in the options, and nothing shows up outside a dungeon unless you want it to.

***

## During a run

* **Key timer:** a movable, resizable timer showing time left, the +2 and +3 timers, this week's affixes, trash count %, boss kills, and deaths (with class colors and the time each death costs).
* **Let me focus:** click the timer or the death count to hide it during a pull, click again to show it.
* **Auto-slot your key:** puts your keystone in the font for you when you open it. Never in combat, and never on top of a key someone else already put in.
* **Enemy nameplates:** turns enemy nameplates on when a key starts, then sets them back how you had them when it ends.
* **Notes:** a note for each dungeon plus a note for each boss, with simple formatting. It can switch to a boss note when you pull it and back after. You can also write your notes ahead of time in the options. Notes are saved and survive reloads and wipes.

## With your group

* **Guild keys:** your guild's best keys this week, shown right inside Blizzard's Mythic+ window. Hover a key to see the group.
* **Party keys:** everyone in the group who has the addon shares their key, so you get a dropdown of all party keys on the Premade Groups create screen. Pick one and it fills in the dungeon and a "+level" title for you.
* **!keys in chat:** type !keys (or anyone in your group can) and the addon links the keystone you're carrying. Works even if the other people don't have the addon.
* **Joined a group:** when you get invited through Group Finder, it shows the dungeon, the leader, the group note, and a teleport button if you have one.
* **Missing buff reminder:** tells you when someone in the group is missing their class buff (only for classes actually in the group). Pick party chat, a popup, or both, choose which buffs to watch, and set how often it reminds you.
* **Bloodlust alert:** a quick on-screen popup when someone types bl, hero, lust, or drums in chat.

## Extras

* **Season Best teleports:** click a dungeon in your Season Best row to teleport there.
* **Group listing defaults:** keeps your difficulty and playstyle set the way you like when you make a group, and warns you if a dungeon isn't set to Mythic.
* **At a vendor:** auto repair (guild funds first if you allow it) and auto sell grey junk.
* **At quest givers:** auto accept and turn in quests, everywhere or only in dungeons. It never clicks a "leave the dungeon" option for you.
* **Chat:** makes links in chat clickable to copy, and adds a Copy button to each chat tab.
* **Minimap button:** opens the options and toggles Let me focus. Drag it to move it, or hide it.
* **Test mode:** show all the movable frames at once so you can place and size them before a run.

***

## Commands

* `/mpt` - open the options
* `/mpt frames` - show or hide the test frames
* `/mpt guild` - show or hide the guild keys panel
* `/mpt lock` - lock or unlock the timer
* `/mpt reset` - put the timer back where it started
